What the whole dataset looks like

Krippendorff's α is the inter-rater reliability of the audio-only condition. The ceiling is the best accuracy any model could reach against a 10-rater consensus label, given how much those raters disagree with each other. Numbers above the ceiling are not skill, they are overfitting to a particular panel's habits.

CREMA-D: Cao et al. 2014, IEEE Trans. Affective Computing 5(4):377-390. Clips and per-rater responses are the authors', reproduced here for illustration. Code and full analysis: emotion-label-ceiling.
